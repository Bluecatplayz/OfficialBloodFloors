About the Game

Step into the abandoned office where every shadow hides a threat. Armed only with your flashlight, navigate dark hallways, flickering lights, and eerie green-tinted rooms. Survive the unknown, uncover hidden secrets, and feel your heart race as the office itself seems alive… and hungry.

Game Features

VR Immersion

Experience fully interactive horror in virtual reality, making every corner terrifying.

Dynamic Flashlight

Toggle your flashlight to explore or survive in pitch-dark areas filled with danger.

Flickering Lights

Uneven office lighting and flickering bulbs heighten tension and keep you on edge.

Abandoned Office

Navigate realistic, creepy office environments with hidden threats around every corner.
